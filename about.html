Reflecta Structures / About Us

We don't just build products. We shape possibilities.

Reflecta Structures brings architecture, performance and wellness together — creating solutions that become part of the spaces people live in, work in, train in and return to.

The idea behind Reflecta

A different way of looking at space.

A building is never experienced as a collection of products. It is experienced as light coming through a window. A door opening onto a garden. The feel of a floor underfoot. The energy of a court. The quiet of a sauna after a long day. That is the thinking behind Reflecta Structures.

We bring together carefully considered architectural systems, sports surfaces, complete sports environments and wellness spaces so that the individual elements work together as one experience.

Where it begins

Architecture with purpose.

Our architectural work is centred around aluminium systems — windows, doors, sliding solutions, large openings, cascading and folding systems, and façade applications.

We look at the opening as more than a frame. It is the point where architecture meets daylight, ventilation, views, movement and the outdoors. Our aim is to help create openings that feel effortless within the architecture around them.

For homeowners, architects, designers and developers, this means approaching each project with the proportions, use, aesthetic and practical requirements of the space in mind.

Performance underfoot

Built for movement.

Sport is different from ordinary flooring. Every step, landing, change of direction and training session places different demands on a surface.

Reflecta brings together sports flooring solutions for gyms, training spaces, recreational environments and specialised applications — including gym flooring, rubber, vinyl, EPDM and wooden sports surfaces.

The objective is simple: create surfaces that belong to the activity, support the space and look considered enough to become part of its overall design.

Beyond the surface

We build the place where sport happens.

A court is more than painted lines. It is an environment with a purpose, a rhythm and an identity.

Sports Courts

From court to complete environment.

We work across lawn tennis, basketball, pickleball, volleyball, box cricket and project-specific sports environments — bringing the surface, layout and visual character together.

Wellness

Spaces designed to slow down.

Steam rooms, saunas and custom wellness spaces are designed around atmosphere, comfort, materials and the rituals of recovery.

“The best spaces don't ask to be noticed. They make you feel the difference.”

Our four worlds

Architecture. Sport. Wellness. Infrastructure.
